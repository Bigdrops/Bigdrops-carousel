Problem Reported
“Steam pressure fluctuations and burner instability during high-demand cycles. Boiler experiencing repeated flame-out events and inconsistent steam output, affecting downstream production throughput. Plant operations curtailed to 55% capacity awaiting resolution.”
Defects Found
  • 01. Defective flame scanner sensor — intermittent signal loss causing burner lockout under modulation.
  • 02. Clogged fuel nozzle assembly — carbon deposits disrupting fuel atomisation; flame pattern distorted.
  • 03. Worn-out gasket on steam drum manhole — minor steam weep at operating pressure (10.5 bar).
  • 04. Calibration drift on pressure controller — indicated reading offset by +0.8 bar vs. reference standard.
Service Rendered
Conducted full diagnostic assessment of boiler control system and burner management controller (BMC). Replaced defective flame scanner sensor and recalibrated BMC modulation parameters. Dismantled, cleaned, and serviced fuel nozzle assembly; decarbonised all burner components. Replaced worn steam drum manhole gasket and pressure controller. Flushed fuel supply lines and replaced both primary and secondary fuel filter elements. Performed combustion efficiency test across firing range; adjusted air-to-fuel ratio for optimal combustion (λ = 1.18). Conducted hydrostatic pressure test at 11.5 bar — zero leakage observed. Full recommissioning test run completed across 30%–100% load range. Steam output stabilised at 6,450 kg/h ±50 kg/h. System handed over in full operational condition.

Engineer Remarks
Root cause traced to flame scanner sensor degradation compounded by carbon fouling in the fuel nozzle assembly. Both primary defects contributed to the instability and flame-out events. We recommend implementing a quarterly preventive maintenance programme covering burner component inspection, nozzle cleaning, and sensor verification. Additionally, feedwater hardness was measured at 8.2 ppm (target: <2 ppm). A water softener system evaluation is advised to forestall future scale accumulation on heat exchange surfaces.
